# Signing in

Open the console URL your administrator gave you and sign in with your **email address and
password**. Accounts are created by an administrator — there is no self-service sign-up unless
your site has enabled it.

![Sign-in screen](/img/screens/login.png)

## First sign-in

The first time you sign in you are asked to **change the password** your administrator set.
The console will not let you go anywhere else until you do. Pick a password only you know;
administrators can reset it but never see it.

## Language and theme

The top bar has two toggles that apply only to your browser:

- **EN / KO** switches the console language. Every screen, message, and error is available in
  both.
- The **moon / sun** toggle switches between the light and dark theme.

## The cluster selector

Next to the sidebar toggle is a pill showing which **cluster** the console is looking at
(*All clusters* by default). It narrows every list, dashboard, and the session wizard's default
to one cluster. Most installations have a single cluster, in which case you can leave it alone —
it is there so the answer to "why do I only see two nodes?" is always visible in the bar.

## Signing out

Open the menu under your name at the bottom of the sidebar and choose **Sign out**. A session
token that expires (or an administrator disabling your account) also signs you out; you simply
sign in again.
